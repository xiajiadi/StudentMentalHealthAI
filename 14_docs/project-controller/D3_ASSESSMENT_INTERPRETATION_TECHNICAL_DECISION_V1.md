8. FROZEN D3I-01 THROUGH D3I-40
======================================================================

D3I-01
D3-2 belongs to module:

03_assessment-adapter

not:

07_prompt-engine.

D3I-02
D3-2 is deterministic.

It MUST NOT call an LLM.

D3I-03
CAT Engine remains the sole computation authority for:

theta
SE
stopping evidence

D3-2 MUST NOT recalculate theta or SE.

D3I-04
D3-2 MUST NOT freely infer psychological meaning from numeric scores.

D3I-05
Every score interpretation must pass through a versioned:

InterpretationPolicyPack

or semantically equivalent typed/versioned policy artifact.

D3I-06
No exact matching valid approved policy:

FAIL CLOSED / ABSTAIN.

No generic/default psychological interpretation fallback.

D3I-07
Every policy pack binds:

instrument_id
instrument_version
population_scope
policy_version
policy_hash

D3I-08
Current product population direction:

university freshmen

Suggested current age range:

17–19

17-year-old participants may be minors.

Existing ethics / consent / privacy gates remain.

D3I-09
Population mismatch prohibits reuse of interpretation policy.

D3I-10
Population expansion requires new validation/review.

Measurement invariance / DIF is an important future validation gate when
comparisons or score interpretation are extended across materially different
groups.

D3I-11
V0.9 prohibits generic:

theta -> mild/moderate/severe

mapping.

D3I-12
V0.9 prohibits Level 1–5 psychological classification.

D3I-13
V0.9 prohibits hard-coded psychological cutoffs.

D3I-14
Future cutoffs/bands require all of:

instrument-specific
population-valid
evidence-backed
human-approved

before entering an interpretation policy.

D3I-15
theta and SE are machine/audit evidence by default.

They MUST NOT be directly forwarded into Prompt Engine renderable claims.

D3I-16
SE represents estimate precision.

SE MUST NOT represent:

psychological risk
diagnostic confidence
crisis probability
clinical severity.

D3I-17
D3-2 MUST NOT independently expose or calculate confidence intervals for user
interpretation unless a future instrument policy explicitly approves the metric,
formula and reporting semantics.

D3I-18
Construct:

name
direction
high-score meaning
low-score meaning

MUST NOT be hard-coded in adapter logic.

They must come from reviewed construct/policy metadata.

D3I-19
Old four-domain names not finally approved by human reconciliation MUST NOT be
silently frozen into D3-2.

D3I-20
D3-2 outputs typed structured claims/status.

It does NOT generate natural-language interpretation prose.

D3I-21
D3-2 output MUST NOT contain:

diagnosis
disorder determination
clinical severity
suicide inference
self-harm inference
treatment decision.

D3I-22
Psychometric interpretation MUST NOT produce Safety actions.

D3I-23
If trusted Safety state is:

CRISIS_ROUTE
REVIEW_REQUIRED
BLOCK

normal interpretation must:

BYPASS / ABSTAIN

but D3-2 MUST NOT change the Safety decision.

D3I-24
D3-2 output is canonical and role-neutral.

Student/Teacher/Parent presentation differences belong to:

D3-3 Role Projection.

D3I-25
Parent production delivery remains PAUSED.

Parent synthetic/demo capability may remain.

D3I-26
D3-2 MUST NOT use the new unreviewed D2 intake:

14 sources / 58 fragments.

D3I-27
Psychometric interpretation provenance and RAG knowledge provenance are separate
provenance domains.

D3-2 MUST NOT depend on RAG to decide score meaning.

D3I-28
Policy packs must record:

evidence_refs
review status
review decision identity
effective policy version.

D3I-29
allowed_claims and forbidden_claims are produced by deterministic policy logic.

They are NOT generated by an LLM.

D3I-30
No approved claim:

Prompt Engine may not invent one later.

D3I-31
D3-2 must provide explicit abstention/bypass statuses and reason codes.

Never use:

empty interpretation
silent fallback
generic default.

D3I-32
Do NOT introduce:

OPA
generic rules engine
DSPy
LangChain
Outlines
other new interpretation runtime frameworks.

D3I-33

typed Python
+
versioned JSON policy
+
module-owned schema
+
deterministic tests.

D3I-34
D3-2 contract is currently module-owned internal contract.

Do NOT modify Frozen Root.

D3I-35
Promotion to Root machine contract is deferred to future D3-4 integration
decision.

D3I-36
Real student data remains prohibited.

D3I-37
REAL_USER interpretation mode remains:

BLOCKED.

D3I-38
V0.9 may support only:

SYNTHETIC_DEMO
RESEARCH_ONLY

within explicitly approved policy scope.

D3I-39
Synthetic personalization / interpretation rules must be explicitly marked
synthetic-only.

They must not be represented as validated psychology interpretation.

D3I-40
D3-2 engineering PASS does NOT imply:

psychometric validity
real pilot readiness
psychology approval
clinical validity
production readiness.
