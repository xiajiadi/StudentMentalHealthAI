# Public-resource candidates for the three-layer RAG

**Research checked:** 2026-10-13
**Status:** `RESEARCH_CANDIDATES_ONLY` — no source is approved, human-curated, or eligible for live retrieval.
**Scope:** Primary and official sources for `policy`, `theory`, and `intervention`. High-school and postsecondary populations remain separate. This is source and license research, not professional content approval, a legal opinion, or authorization to use real student data.

## Decision boundary

This list identifies material whose publisher or rights page states a reuse basis. A reuse license does not establish psychological appropriateness, measurement validity, school adoption, or the right to present guidance as professional advice. The research team must still confirm project-purpose fit, jurisdiction, attribution and third-party exclusions. A qualified education/mental-health reviewer must separately review user-facing content.

“Stage” below means an isolated external research copy/metadata record only. It does not mean the Knowledge Pipeline may ingest it, `rights_status=verified`, `review_status=approved`, `human_curated=true`, or corpus publication. Do not copy a source into the active or synthetic G corpus. Keep source-derived text and AI-generated summaries distinguishable, retain canonical URL/version/attribution, and do not publish derived text until rights and content reviews are complete.

Population labels are source-specific. In particular, `university_student` is not interchangeable with `university_freshman`; a broad youth age band does not prove a university setting was studied. Store setting, age/grade, country, role, publication date, and source limitations alongside every candidate.

The candidates below are guidance, reviews, or aggregate institutional research. They do **not** provide an 800-person respondent dataset or a public question bank. The DfE higher-education survey is institution-level; it is not student microdata. PLOS article pages may link to anonymized research data, but this task did not download those files. Do not turn aggregate counts into synthetic-looking individual responses or imply that any synthetic cohort represents a real school population.

## Held source: useful content with unresolved rights boundary

**WHO Helping Adolescents Thrive (HAT) guideline** — [WHO publication record](https://www.who.int/publications/b/55043), [PDF](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/mental-health/guidelines-on-mental-health-promotive-and-preventive-interventions-for-adolescents-hat.pdf). WHO, 2020; adolescents defined as ages 10–19; global policy/evidence guidance. The publication lists CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO (noncommercial use; attribution; ShareAlike for adaptations), but the same PDF front matter also calls this an “advanced proof” that must not be quoted, reproduced, translated, or adapted without WHO permission. That is a material internal license conflict. **Metadata only; do not stage the PDF/full text or derive RAG chunks until the rights owner clarifies the controlling notice and the project confirms noncommercial purpose.** Even after rights clarification, intervention content needs specialist review.
